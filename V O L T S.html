2‑player neon‑yellow infinite runner. Tight platforming, double jumps, speed pads, jump pads, spikes. Survive together as the world accelerates.
P1: Jump = W (double jump enabled). Drop = S.
P2: Jump = ↑ (double jump enabled). Drop = ↓.

Jump Pad: launches you high; chain into double jump.
Tip: you can “drop” through thin platforms by holding drop and jumping.
